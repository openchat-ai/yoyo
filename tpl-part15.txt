#### What NOT to claim

YOYO is **not** "100% secure" (no software is) · "bug-free" · "faster than C" (slower; state machine) · "easier to use" · "production-ready for general use" (niche).

YOYO **is**: auditable in 1 day · self-hosting (M0→M3≡M3_asm) · cross-verified (3-chain DDC) · backed by documented threat model · frozen after Phase 4d.

### 15.7 Cross-Platform Reality

"YOYO supports any OS and any hardware" — **Designed yes, implemented: 1 CPU + 2 OS today** (Win32 + Linux on x86-64; bare-metal Part 7.7 done but not phase-validated).

Effort to add: Android (0.5 PM) · FreeBSD (1 PM) · macOS (1-2 PM) · ARM64 emit (6-12 PM) · RISC-V emit (6-12 PM) · Bare-metal (1-2 PM).

`0xA1 RAW_BYTES` lets you emit any x64 instruction from within `yoyo.ty`. **YOYO can already express any x64 sequence** — for OS development, you don't need new ISA instructions.
